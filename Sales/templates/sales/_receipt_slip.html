This slip is a BILLING STATEMENT — a BIR "supplementary document" (RR 18-2012), the same class as an order slip or statement of account, NOT a principal tax document. A supplementary document can carry amounts (including the VAT breakdown above when the seller is VAT-registered) but is NEVER valid proof of input tax for the buyer, regardless of what it shows — so it MUST bear that exact marking. The "NOT VALID FOR CLAIM OF INPUT TAX" line is the BIR-standard phrase and applies to every supplementary doc (VAT or not); it matters most once VAT figures print, since that's exactly who might try to claim it. The principal document is the seller's BIR Sales Invoice (their manual booklet in internal mode; the SI- invoice once accredited). {% endcomment %}
